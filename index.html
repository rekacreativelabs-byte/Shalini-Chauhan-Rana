Dr. Shalini Rana, the founder of NutriMind, brings together a decade of experience as a Clinical Nutritionist, Exercise Specialist and Counseling Psychologist — a rare combination that lets her treat the whole person, not just a symptom.

NutriMind isn't a clinic in the ordinary sense. Every plan here is personal, scientific and sustainable: feel the body right, move with purpose, and calm the mind. It's a movement toward complete mind–body wellness, led by a doctor who genuinely cares.

Clinical Nutrition & Therapeutic Diets

Food is your first medicine. Every diet is tailored to your body's biochemistry, lifestyle and emotional needs — helping you heal naturally and sustainably, never through a crash diet.

  • Weight management — sustainable, metabolism-boosting plans for fat loss or gain, without crash diets.
  • PCOS/PCOD & hormonal balance — root-cause healing to regulate cycles, improve skin and balance hormones.
  • Thyroid health — customized diets that support metabolism, energy and weight regulation.
  • Diabetes & hypertension management — therapeutic diets for blood sugar and heart health.
  • Fatty liver & gut health — liver-detox and microbiome-focused nutrition.
  • Fertility & IVF support diets — nutrient-rich plans that support conception and implantation.
